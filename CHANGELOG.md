# Changelog

All notable changes to SemCred-FL research software are recorded here. Versions
follow semantic versioning for the software implementation; they do not indicate
completion or acceptance of the associated manuscript.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-04

### Added

- Leakage-aware dataset auditing, canonicalization, splitting, preprocessing, and
  federated client partitioning for CICIoT2023 and Edge-IIoTset.
- Deterministic detector training and federated simulation with checkpoint resume
  and hash-chained round records.
- Frozen attack contracts and implementations for data- and update-poisoning
  families.
- Offline semantic-prior construction and validation.
- Semantic and numerical credibility scoring, disagreement-aware fusion,
  persistent trust, eligibility filtering, clipping, and aggregation.
- FedAvg, coordinate median, trimmed mean, and Multi-Krum baselines.
- Evaluation, efficiency, reporting, and paired statistical utilities.
- Resumable Phase 12 clean-matrix plan, execution, audit, and finalization commands.
- GitHub and Zenodo metadata, release audit, CI, and public-release documentation.

### Evidence boundary

- The full confirmatory attacked matrix, ablation study, sensitivity analysis,
  efficiency campaign, registry freeze, and final test evaluation remain pending.
- No synthetic fixture result is designated as measured experimental evidence.
